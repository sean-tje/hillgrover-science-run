How to play

  • Tap anywhere on the game (or press Space) to jump. You can jump twice in the air.
  • Collect coins. Bump an orange ? block from below. Most give a question. Some throw out coins or a booster. A few drop an angry mushroom.
  • Jump over roaming mushrooms and floor spikes, or land on a mushroom to squash it. Touching one costs you 3 coins.
  • Right answer: coins, points and a streak bonus. Every 3 in a row gives a Shield, or a Plane that flies you over danger and hits blocks for you.
  • Wrong answer: you lose 3 coins and see the right answer. You never get knocked out.
  • Missed questions come back later. Clear every question block, reach the flag and your bear earns another chevron.
